ADOPTION TRAJECTORY
Cumulative EV registrations reached 2.07M units across FY22–FY24, with FY24 exceeding 1M annual registrations.
GEOGRAPHIC CONCENTRATION
The top five states — Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, and Rajasthan — account for 60.06% of total EV registrations.

2-Wheelers represent 92.60% of recorded EV registrations (1.91M units), compared with 7.40% for 4-Wheelers.
